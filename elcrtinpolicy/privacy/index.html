Privacy Policy

StocksalesApp is designed around local business records. This policy explains what stays on your device, what may be sent when connected features are used, and the choices you have.

1. At a glance

StocksalesApp is desktop inventory and sales software. Product, sales, customer, and business records are primarily saved on the computer where the App runs. We do not remotely open your device to inspect, correct, or edit those local records.

Some features do communicate with the App’s local server or outside service providers. For example, the App can create a device backup on its local server, send usage-event details to an analytics service when configured, or send an email you request. The sections below explain these cases.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on which features you use, the App can store information in local app files and browser storage on your device. This may include:

  • products, quantities, prices, categories, and stock history;
  • sales, receipts, refunds, customer names or credit-sale details, and expenses;
  • business name, contact details, currency, user profiles, preferences, reminders, and reports;
  • license, setup, language, email-schedule, backup, and other app configuration information;
  • images or attachments that you add, plus local app and device identifiers.

The actual information depends on your use and configuration. Anyone with access to your computer account or local network may be able to access information made available through that device or a shared server. Use device-level security and appropriate access controls.

We do not correct or alter your stored records on your behalf. We do not review or edit the business data kept on your device. The App itself will save changes when you choose actions such as recording a sale, editing stock, or changing settings. You remain responsible for reviewing your records and making backups.

3. Automatic device backups

The App can send a backup of browser local-storage data to the local server associated with your StocksalesApp installation. The backup includes a device identifier, device name/platform, timestamp, and the collected storage data. The local server saves this file in its app-data folder under MEMORY/device-backups. When a newer backup for that device is saved, the server removes its earlier device-backup files.

This is an on-device or local-network backup mechanism, not by itself an external cloud backup. In a shared setup, the data travels to and is stored on the computer hosting the local server. The backup routine skips storage keys whose names contain “password” or “token”; because this is based on key names, it is not a guarantee that all sensitive information is excluded. Treat the backup as private and protect the host computer and network.

Separate manual backups and optional storage integrations may have different locations and retention. Check the destination and provider before using them.

4. Usage diagnostics and device information

When analytics credentials and a connection are available, the App may send usage events to its configured Supabase analytics service. Events observed in the App include startup or dashboard activity and feature actions such as recording a sale, printing a receipt, or activating a license mode. Depending on the event, the information can include:

  • a device identifier (from the computer or a locally generated browser fingerprint);
  • the event name and associated event details;
  • an App username, if available for that event;
  • browser or device user-agent information and App version;
  • approximate location details such as country, city, and coordinates, when available.

For server-side tracking, the server also records a SHA-256 hash of the connecting IP address and may use an IP geolocation lookup when location details are missing. Analytics may be disabled if the service is not configured or available, but this depends on the App’s deployment and settings.

5. Online, email, and license features

If you choose to use an online feature, the relevant information is sent to the service needed to provide it. Depending on the deployment and feature, providers may include Supabase for analytics, Resend for email delivery, IP-API for server-side IP geolocation, and the provider used for license or optional storage features. Examples include:

  • Email and verification: an email address and the message or code needed to send verification, welcome, license, low-stock, or scheduled reports through the configured email-delivery provider. Reports can include business information such as sales or stock details. A verification request may also include device/browser details and an approximate location derived from network or location information.
  • License and activation: information needed to check or manage a license or activation. The App may use a device identifier and activation details for those workflows.
  • Network operation: when you run or share the App’s server on a local network, the server handles requests from connected devices and stores supported records on the hosting computer.
  • Optional backup destinations: if you choose to use an external storage integration, the backup may be transmitted to that provider and be governed by its policies.

Online services may receive network information such as an IP address as a normal part of connecting. The availability and privacy practices of providers, including email, analytics, license, geolocation, and storage providers, are governed by those providers’ own notices and terms.

6. How information may be shared

We do not remotely access your local business records for review or correction. Information may nevertheless be transmitted in the circumstances described above, including to:

  • the local server computer you use for the App’s device-backup or shared-network features;
  • configured service providers that deliver email, analytics, license, geolocation, or optional storage functionality;
  • public authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

We do not describe the local server backup as an external cloud service. Review any connected service’s privacy notice before submitting information to it.

7. Storage and retention

Local app records remain on your device until you or someone with access removes or replaces them, or the App’s own operation changes them. Device backups are stored on the local server computer; when it writes a newer backup for the same device, the server removes earlier matching backup files. Other backups may remain until you delete them.

Information sent to an outside provider is retained according to the provider’s settings, services, and retention practices. The App does not provide a single remote control panel that deletes information from every provider.

8. Your choices and control

  • Use offline workflows where available and avoid optional online features you do not need.
  • Control access to the computer and local network, and use the App’s backup settings thoughtfully.
  • Review, export, or delete local files using the App’s available features and your operating system. Take a verified backup before deleting or changing files.
  • For email communications, use the unsubscribe option where one is provided, or contact support.
  • For information held by a third-party provider, use that provider’s privacy tools or contact it directly.

We do not remotely inspect or edit local records when you contact support. Please do not email sensitive customer or sales data unless it is necessary and you have taken appropriate precautions.

9. Security

We use the App’s local-storage and service features to support its functionality, but no software, device, transmission, or backup method is perfectly secure. The App’s local data files may be readable by users or programs with access to the device. Protect your operating-system account, use trusted networks, restrict who can access shared servers, and keep independent backups of important information.

If you believe information has been exposed through a service we operate, contact us promptly with enough detail to investigate. Do not include passwords or unnecessary customer records.

10. Children and policy changes

The App is intended for business and shop management, not for children. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Policy when the App or its integrations change. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the App or its release information where appropriate.
