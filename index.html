FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy special hardware?

No! StocksalesApp works on any computer, laptop, or tablet. It's compatible with all standard USB/Bluetooth barcode scanners and receipt printers you may already own.

Is my business data secure?

Absolutely. We use encrypted passwords (bcrypt), device authorization limits, role-based staff permission, and secure cloud backups. Your data is isolated per business and never shared.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you can export all your data (sales, stock, customers) to Excel whenever you want.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Sign up and get full access to all Professional features for 7 days — no credit card required. At the end of your trial, choose the plan that fits your business.

Can multiple staff members use the same account?

Yes. Each plan allows multiple devices and users. You can create staff accounts with different permission levels (Admin, Manager, Sales Associate) to control exactly what each team member can access.
