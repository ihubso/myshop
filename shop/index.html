        Security Policy

        Active Security Protocols

        System Monitoring is active. Accounts will be automatically locked after 5 failed login attempts to prevent unauthorized access.

        🔒 Data Privacy & Ownership

        Your Data, Your Control. All business records, inventory, sales, and personal information stored in this system belong exclusively to you, the account owner. Your data is hosted on Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure with enterprise-grade encryption.

        Zero-Knowledge Architecture. Your passwords are encrypted and never stored in plain text. System developers and administrators cannot access, view, or modify your private business records. Only users you authorize with valid credentials can access your business data.

        Cloud-Synced & Protected. Your data is automatically backed up and synced across your devices. Even if you lose your device, your business data remains safe and accessible from any device by logging into your account. You can request complete data deletion at any time.

        Compliance. We follow international data protection standards including GDPR principles. Your data is stored on EU-based servers with Supabase, ensuring the highest level of privacy and security compliance.
