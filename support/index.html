How We Can Help

Our support team covers a wide range of topics. Here are some common areas we assist with:

  • Account & billing — subscription changes, invoicing, payment issues, and refund requests.
  • Technical issues — login problems, slow performance, error messages, and integration troubleshooting.
  • Feature guidance — help with inventory management, POS setup, reporting, and custom configurations.
  • Data & privacy — questions about data handling, export requests, and account deletion.
  • Feedback & suggestions — we love hearing your ideas to make StocksalesApp even better.

📌 Before contacting us: Please check our FAQ and User Guides — many common questions are already answered there. This helps us serve you faster.

Priority Support

For urgent issues that significantly impact your business operations, we offer priority support. Please mark your email as "URGENT" in the subject line and include a brief description of the impact. Our team will escalate your case and respond as quickly as possible.

Enterprise customers with dedicated support plans can reach their account manager directly via the contact details provided in their welcome package.
